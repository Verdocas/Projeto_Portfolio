Hi! I’m Pedro,

  I’m a geographer by training with a profound passion
for urban planning and sustainable practices.
As a society I believe we must strive to move our
systems into the future, however, that won’t happen as
long as we keep acting with the same mindset that
originated the challenges we face today. My mission,
both personal and professional, is to challenge said
mindsets in order to build more robust and integrated
communities within reach of current circumstances.

I’ve graduated from college in 2024 and am currently seeking experience in the field.
Although I lack the experience, I believe to be highly adaptable to any position and a
dedicated professional, corroborated by my involvement as a volunteer with several
institutions and projects of different affairs.
As for my personal attributes and charisma, I’m a curious person, with a childlike fascination
for nature and the arts, I find something very central to us a species in the contemplative
aspect of both and important to me as a geographer, I’m also creative and crafty, finding joy
in unorthodox solutions for hopeless problems with minimum resources. Lastly I’d like to point
out my perfectionism, both as a virtue and a flaw, I enjoy doing things properly and
thoroughly, I don’t like to take corners and
appreciate the little details, however, my obsession turns
into a trap when I inevitably feel overwhelmed by the pressure of doing everything in a given
time window, I guess wouldn’t be human without a flaw, but I believe to be changing for the
better as life as proven me wrong on this matter.
